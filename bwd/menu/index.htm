A Little Bit of History

Tim's Pizza, located on Main Street in Olivet, Michigan, has been a beloved
community hub for over three decades. Known for its signature breadsticks and fresh pizzas,
the restaurant was originally owned by Scott Ramage, an Olivet College alumnus, who
established it as a local favorite in the 1990s. After Scott's retirement in March 2024,
the restaurant temporarily closed, leaving the community eager for its return. In July 2024, Kim Haver acquired Tim's Pizza and reopened it in October 2024. Haver has
maintained the cherished recipes and traditions while introducing updates like extended
hours and plans for online ordering. The restaurant remains a cornerstone of the community,
welcoming both loyal patrons and new visitors to enjoy its iconic offerings and build new
memories.
