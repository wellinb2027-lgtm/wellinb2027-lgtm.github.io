Matt - Director

Matt is the director of our theatre team. He is responsible for organizing rehearsals, helping actors understand their characters, and bringing the entire production together. He loves storytelling and working with creative people.

Jace - Set Designer

Jace designs and builds the scenery used in our shows. He enjoys creating realistic environments and finding creative ways to transform the stage. His favorite part of theatre is seeing a design come to life.

Aiden - Lighting Designer

Aiden is in charge of designing the lighting for each production. He uses different colors, effects, and lighting positions to help create the mood of every scene. He enjoys the technical side of theatre.

Ben - Sound Designer

Ben handles the sound effects, music, and microphones for our productions. He makes sure every sound is clear and happens at the right time. He enjoys working behind the scenes to make performances successful.
