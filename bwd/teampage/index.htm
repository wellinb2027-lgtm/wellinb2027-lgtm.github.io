Emily Johnson - Director

Emily is the director of our theatre team. She is responsible for organizing rehearsals, helping actors understand their characters, and bringing the entire production together. She loves storytelling and working with creative people.

Michael Davis - Set Designer

Michael designs and builds the scenery used in our shows. He enjoys creating realistic environments and finding creative ways to transform the stage. His favorite part of theatre is seeing a design come to life.

Sarah Williams - Lighting Designer

Sarah is in charge of designing the lighting for each production. She uses different colors, effects, and lighting positions to help create the mood of every scene. She enjoys the technical side of theatre.

James Wilson - Sound Designer

James handles the sound effects, music, and microphones for our productions. He makes sure every sound is clear and happens at the right time. He enjoys working behind the scenes to make performances successful.
