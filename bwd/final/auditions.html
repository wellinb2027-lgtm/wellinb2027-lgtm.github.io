Auditions & getting involved

Auditions are an opportunity to share your preparation, curiosity, and willingness to collaborate.

Prepare with confidence

  • Read the audition notice carefully and note the date, time, and location.
  • Choose material that fits the instructions and practice it aloud.
  • Wear comfortable clothing that allows you to move safely.
  • Arrive with enough time to check in and get settled.
  • Listen to directions and be respectful of everyone auditioning.

Important: This is a general guide, not an active audition announcement. Confirm current requirements with the theatre director or school.

More than one way to participate

Acting

Explore characters, scenes, and storytelling.

Music & movement

Support a production through singing, rhythm, and choreography.

Production crew

Help with scenery, costumes, props, lighting, sound, or stage management.
