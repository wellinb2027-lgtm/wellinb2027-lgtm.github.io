Backstage & technical theatre

Technical theatre combines creativity with practical problem-solving. The audience may not see every detail, but they experience the work in every scene.

Set design & construction

Plan the physical world of the story, considering sightlines, movement, materials, and scene changes.

Lighting design

Use light, color, and timing to guide attention, establish mood, and help audiences understand the setting.

Sound design

Shape the sound world with music, effects, microphones, and careful levels so the story can be heard clearly.
