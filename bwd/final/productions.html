Our productions

Every show is a collaboration. It grows through rehearsal, design, construction, and teamwork.

How a show comes together

  1. Choose the story. Consider the cast, audience, resources, and creative goals.
  2. Plan the production. Create a schedule and concepts for scenery, costumes, sound, and lighting.
  3. Rehearse and build. Actors develop their roles while crews prepare the world of the play.
  4. Perform and reflect. Share the work with an audience and celebrate the whole team.
