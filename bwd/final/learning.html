SKILLS THAT GO BEYOND THE STAGE

Learn by creating

Theatre gives students a place to practice creative thinking, communication, responsibility, and teamwork through hands-on projects.

Skills you can develop

  • Collaboration and respectful communication
  • Time management and preparation
  • Creative thinking and adaptability
  • Public speaking and confidence
  • Technical problem-solving
  • Leadership and responsibility

These skills can support future interests in education, engineering, design, media, and communication.

Ways to keep learning

Observe

Notice how lighting, sound, movement, and scenery work together in a performance.

Ask questions

Talk with directors, designers, crew members, and performers about their process.

Reflect

Identify what worked, what challenged you, and what you would try next.
